# Flamingo sprite artwork

Created with the built-in image generation tool. Transparent PNG source files are preserved; the application samples their alpha as a solid black silhouette. No procedural wing deformation is applied to these images.

Eight takeoff pages contain 32 sequential poses; four flight pages contain 16 poses for one wingbeat. Each source page has a 2 × 2 layout. `sprites.json` records actual rectangles and torso anchors, so uneven generated margins do not clip the bird or move its registration point.

| Heart pair | [couple.png](couple.png) | 1 static pair |

The original PNGs are unmodified. Takeoff pages C and D are interleaved to keep the rising wing continuous. Extended-flight frames use the unoccluded head to stabilize registration; water-contact points are measured at the bottom of the grounded poses. Crops, anchors and playback order are stored in the manifest rather than painted into the images.

The dense studies in `studies/` were used as anatomical and timing references. The source material was generated specifically for this project. The earlier page with a defective background was discarded.
